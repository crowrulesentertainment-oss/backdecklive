Back Deck Live is a relaxed entertainment and community show from Tacoma, Washington.

What's Back Deck Live?

Think of a conversation on a back deck: comfortable, honest, welcoming and open to different points of view. The show brings people together to talk about everyday experiences, opinions, stories, community and the things that make life interesting.

Made in the Pacific Northwest

Back Deck Live starts in Tacoma and the surrounding Pacific Northwest, with an open invitation to viewers and guests wherever they call home.

A CrowRules Entertainment production

Back Deck Live is part of CrowRules Entertainment, an independent entertainment company being built in Tacoma, Washington.
