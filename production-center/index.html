Fallback procedures

If the primary stream fails

  1. Producer calls a brief hold and confirms whether the issue is local, encoder-side or platform-side.
  2. Check network connectivity, OBS output status, dropped frames, encoder load and platform stream health.
  3. If the primary path cannot recover promptly, announce a short technical break; do not expose private dashboards or credentials.
  4. Switch to the pre-tested backup encoder / network / stream destination only after confirming the backup is configured and authorized.
  5. Verify audio and video on the backup destination, then tell the audience the stream has resumed.
  6. If no safe backup is available, stop the stream cleanly, publish an update through the approved CrowRules channels and reschedule.
Never share stream keys, passwords, private RTMP URLs or account recovery codes in this panel or in producer notes. Store credentials only in the approved password manager / secure platform settings.
